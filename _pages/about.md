I am a 4th-year Ph.D. candidate at the School of Software Engineering, Sun Yat-sen University, supervised by [Prof. Zibin Zheng](http://www.zibinzheng.com/cv) and [Jiachi Chen](https://jiachi-chen.github.io/) at [InPlusLab](http://www.inpluslab.com/) since September 2022. I expect to graduate in June 2027.

My research sits at **program analysis**, **AI for software security**, and **Web3 security**, with a particular focus on uncovering vulnerabilities in smart contracts and blockchain clients. I also work on **AI security** and adversarial risks in LLM-powered systems.

I have published **8 papers** at top-tier (CCF-A) venues including **ICSE**, **ISSTA**, **USENIX Security**, **TOSEM**, **TSE**, and **TIFS**. Beyond academia, I actively hunt real-world bugs and write PoCs. I maintain [![X Views](https://img.shields.io/badge/X%2FTwitter_Defi__Nerd__sec-300k%2B%20views-white?logo=x&style=social&logoColor=gray)](https://x.com/Defi_Nerd_sec), and develop [DarkNavySecurity/web3-skills](https://github.com/DarkNavySecurity/web3-skills) ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/DarkNavySecurity/web3-skills).
